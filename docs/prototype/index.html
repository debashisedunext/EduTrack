Two recommendations to review. The blueprint specifies a donut for task-type distribution; with 11 categories a donut is very hard to read, so this shows horizontal bars instead — same data, ranked, directly labelled. Separately, the §12.1 chart palette is labelled colour-blind safe but does not pass: teal and pink are indistinguishable for deuteranopes, and green and cyan are close even in full colour. The charts here use a validated subset. A corrected eight-hue set is in the handover notes.
